About DAAT

Building financial infrastructure for a more trusted economy.

Who we are

A Malawian financial and technology company

DAAT Limited is a Malawian financial and technology company focused on expanding access to finance and building digital trust infrastructure.

Our approach combines responsible lending, verification, trusted data and technology to help individuals and businesses participate more effectively in the formal financial economy.

Trust, built layer by layer

How we work

Two disciplines, one company

DAAT Finance

Responsible financing for businesses and organised groups, assessed on real contracts, payroll cycles and group guarantees.

DAATECH Digital Trust

Digital identity, verification and consent-based data sharing that gives people and businesses a trusted, portable record.
