How it works

From application to disbursement

  1. Apply

    Tell us about your business, group or contract, and the financing you need.

  2. Assessment

    We review your application against our lending criteria. This may include a request for supporting documents or a Trust Check.

  3. Decision

    We confirm whether your application is approved, and on what terms. Submitting an application is not a guarantee of approval.

  4. Disbursement

    Approved financing is disbursed according to the agreed terms and repayment schedule.

Applying for finance does not guarantee approval. All applications are subject to assessment against DAAT's lending criteria.
